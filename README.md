# Awesome-Digital-Credentialing

# Top Digital Credentialing Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Open Badges, Digital Certificates, Micro-Credentials, Verifiable Learning Records & Skills Recognition*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Credentialing**. These systems issue, store, and verify badges and certificates so learners can prove skills and achievements across institutions and employers.

**Examples** include Credly, Badgr, Accredible, Canvas Credentials, Certifier, Certopus, Virtualbadge.io, Open Badge Factory, CanCred, and VerifyEd (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Credentialing is standards-rich. **Open Badges 3.0**, **LearnCard**, **Digital Credentials Consortium** tooling, and **W3C Verifiable Credentials** stacks enable open issuance and wallets. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Credly, Badgr, Accredible](https://www.credly.com/)**  
  Leading digital badge and certificate platforms used by universities, training providers, and professional bodies.

- **[Canvas Credentials, Open Badge Factory, CanCred](https://www.instructure.com/)**  
  LMS-adjacent and European Open Badges platforms for institutional credential programs.

- **[Certifier, Certopus, Virtualbadge.io, VerifyEd](https://certifier.io/)**  
  Modern certificate and badge issuers for courses, events, and workforce programs.

- **[Other commercial digital credentialing platforms](https://www.credly.com/)**  
  Additional micro-credential and skills-passport services.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[LearnCard](https://github.com/learningeconomy)**  
  Open-source wallet, SDK, and network for issuing W3C Verifiable Credentials / Open Badges 3.0 that learners own and share.

- **[Digital Credentials Consortium (DCC) tooling](https://github.com/digitalcredentials)**  
  Open issuer, verifier, and wallet-related projects for academic digital credentials aligned with modern VC standards.

- **[Open Badges specification & validators](https://www.imsglobal.org/activity/digital-badges)**  
  1EdTech Open Badges standard (v2/v3)—the interoperable foundation most platforms implement (v3 aligns with W3C VC).

- **[Badgr open-source history / community forks](https://github.com/search?q=badgr+open+badges)**  
  Community and historical open Badgr-related codebases for Open Badges issuance (verify current license and maintenance).

- **[Veramo / walt.id credential stacks](https://github.com/decentralized-identity/veramo)**  
  Open SSI frameworks used to build custom credential issuers and wallets beyond education-specific products.

- **[Blockcerts](https://github.com/blockchain-certificates)**  
  Open standard and tools for blockchain-anchored certificates (still used in some credential programs).

- **[Moodle / open LMS badge plugins](https://github.com/moodle/moodle)**  
  Open learning platforms with native or plugin support for Open Badges issuance.

- **[Credential verification libraries](https://github.com/search?q=open+badges+verify+OR+verifiable+credentials+verify)**  
  Community verifiers and parsers for Open Badges and W3C VCs.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Learner-owned credentials**: LearnCard and DCC wallet patterns.
- **Standards**: Issue Open Badges 3.0 / W3C VCs for maximum portability.
- **LMS integration**: Moodle or other open LMS badge features.
- **Composable stacks**: Open LMS → Open Badges issuer → LearnCard/DCC wallet → public verifier.
- Commercial platforms still lead in design templates, labor-market partnerships, and institutional analytics.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
**Open Badges 3.0** + **LearnCard** / **DCC** / **Veramo** for open issuance and holding.  
Commercial platforms (Credly, Accredible, Badgr, Canvas Credentials, etc.) provide polished issuer UX and discovery networks.  
Educators can run open badge programs; large credential marketplaces often choose commercial hosts. Fully open digital credentialing is viable when standards compliance is maintained.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Digital credentials are only as trustworthy as their issuers and verification methods. Prefer standards-based (Open Badges 3.0 / W3C VC) credentials and protect signing keys. Misrepresentation of achievements can harm learners and employers.
- Open-source stacks offer portability and transparency but require operational care. Commercial platforms shift hosting and support to the vendor. Neither replaces rigorous assessment design and honest credential criteria.
